# Financial Modeling & Equity Valuation Portfolio
A collection of financial valuation models, DCF analysis, and equity research spreadsheets for key Indian companies across IT, Retail, and Banking sectors.
## Repository Structure & Models
* **Trent Ltd:** `Trent.xlsx` — Retail financial metrics and performance analysis.
* **Tata Consultancy Services (TCS):** `TCS.xlsx` — IT services financial evaluation and historical trend analysis.
* **Mphasis:** `Mphasis.xlsx`, `Mphasis Fin Analysis.xlsx` — Core financial model and valuation breakdown.
* **Indian Bank:** `Indian Bank.xlsx`, `Indian bank DCF modelling.xlsx` — Banking sector DCF valuation and credit metrics.
* **Valuation Template:** `DCF modelling.xlsx` — Reusable Discounted Cash Flow valuation framework.

## Analytical Scope
* **DCF Modeling:** Free Cash Flow to Firm (FCFF) and banking-specific cash flow projections.
* **Financial Analysis:** Revenue growth trends, margin tracking, and leverage/profitability ratios.
